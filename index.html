How BZAT Rewards Works

  • Rewards are based on genuine qualifying BZAT.ai purchases.
  • There is no reward simply for signing up or recruiting someone.
  • Maximum reward depth is two levels.
  • Rewards initially enter Pending status.
  • A 7-day verification/hold period applies.
  • After successful verification, eligible rewards become Available.
  • Refunds or chargebacks can reverse related rewards.
  • Minimum withdrawal amount is $10.
  • Initial withdrawals may use Bank Transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa with manual verification.
  • Self-referrals are prohibited.
  • Duplicate accounts or suspicious activity may be reviewed or blocked.
Important: BZAT Rewards does not pay users simply for recruiting members. Rewards are generated from qualifying purchases of genuine BZAT.ai products and services. Rewards are not guaranteed income and are not an investment.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is BZAT.ai?

BZAT.ai is an AI-powered content creation platform designed to turn one idea into multiple forms of content.

Is BZAT.ai free to start?

Yes. BZAT.ai is designed to offer a free starting experience, with paid options for additional usage.

How does BZAT Rewards work?

BZAT Rewards is a product-based referral program. Eligible rewards come from qualifying purchases, not simply from recruiting or signing up members.

How many referral levels are there?

The planned reward structure has a maximum of two levels: 10% for direct qualifying purchases and 3% for qualifying purchases at the second level.

When can rewards be withdrawn?

Eligible rewards must pass the verification period and the planned minimum withdrawal threshold is $10.

Is BZAT Token available now?

No. BZAT Token is marked as Coming Later. Any future launch details will be announced separately.
